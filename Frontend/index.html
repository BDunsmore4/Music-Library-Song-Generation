About this map

7,000+ tracks from my Spotify library, embedded using LAION-AI/CLAP, a deep learning model that combines natural language and audio. Then reduced to 2D with UMAP.

Red dots are tracks generated by ElevenLabs Music; purple dots are tracks searched from Apple Music — each plotted by its acoustic embedding.

Built to explore my music library, and how AI-generated music fits amongst it.

Future directions include algorithms to generate prompts that create novel additions to the music library.
